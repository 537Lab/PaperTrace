## 3. Design revision driven by test evidence (#43 → #49 → #50)

A replay against #43 `c36ad0bffb5b7e7d0465e0b7309e14d2098c400d` reported the same evidence ID twice. D rejected the draft with `RESULT_SHAPE_INVALID`, but the failed call returned no result object, so the attempt count that had just been observed was lost. The refusal was correct; the diagnostic record was not.

The repair was re-scoped as a test-first change. Six cases were approved on #49 before any source edit:

| Approved case | What it pins | Result on #43 | Result on #50 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `test_rejected_gateway_result_retains_valid_accounting[observed]` | one observed attempt survives rejection | failed (lost) | passed |
| `...[zero]` | an explicit zero is not confused with unknown | failed (lost) | passed |
| `...[unknown]` | `None` survives as unknown | failed (lost) | passed |
| `test_rejected_gateway_result_does_not_invent_accounting[absent]` | absent accounting stays absent | passed (control) | passed |
| `...[boolean-count]` | `True` is not accepted as a count | passed (control) | passed |
| `...[blank-provider]` | blank provider is rejected | passed (control) | passed |

The design change was not to weaken the rejection. #50 keeps `RESULT_SHAPE_INVALID` and empty body, sent and cited sets, and moves the validated provider, model and attempt count into a separate `outcome.accounting` field. The three valid-accounting cases that failed on #43 pass on #50, and the three controls still pass. The E-side replay then preserved `1`, `0` and `null` through save, reopen and default export.
