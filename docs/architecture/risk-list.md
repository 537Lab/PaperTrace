## Usage policy

On a trigger, carry out the corresponding stop/isolate/refuse action first, then form an ADR or a verification task; no risk in the table is marked closed. No weighted numbers such as “5% probability, impact 10 points” are used without real verification. The evidence that a risk has been eliminated should be written into [evaluation-logbook.md](evaluation-logbook.md), and whether to close it is decided by real execution and student review, not by the model declaring the risk accepted.
